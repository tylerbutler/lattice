## v2.0.1 - 2026-11-18

### Fixed

#### Keep appended and replacement text at the requested position after moves
#### Prevent JavaScript stack overflows in large text edits and reads

Text inherits the sequence fixes for deletion, moves, reads and edits after moves, and compaction in large documents. Public APIs and snapshot formats are unchanged.
#### Reject duplicate identities in decoded text

`from_json` now rejects repeated character IDs, overlapping compact blocks, duplicate forwarding keys, and IDs that are both retained and forwarded. This prevents character loss during merge. The checks apply to v1 and v2 snapshots; sparse text deltas remain valid.
